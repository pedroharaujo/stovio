Privacy policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

This policy explains what personal information the Stovio Android app and its services handle, why, who receives it, how long it is kept, and the choices and rights you have.

Stovio is currently being tested. Some retention limits and provider settings are still under review. The sections below explain current handling and the limits we are working toward.

Who we are

Stovio is operated by Pedro Henrique Araujo Pinto, acting as an individual, who is responsible for your personal information ("we", "us").

Contact for privacy questions and requests: pharaujo1094@gmail.com

What Stovio does

Stovio lets you watch short vertical drama series. You need to sign in to open Home and Wallet, buy coins and unlock paid episodes. Some episodes are free; others are unlocked with coins that you buy through Google Play. Stovio does not show advertising.

Information we handle

Account and sign-in

You sign in with an email address and password or with your Google account. Google Firebase Authentication handles your sign-in details. Our own servers receive a Firebase account identifier and store it with a random profile identifier, account dates, your language, an optional country and your privacy choices. Our servers do not store your password.

Viewing activity

We store which series and episodes you watch, your playback position, whether you finished an episode, the episodes you have unlocked, related dates and times, and whether you turned on automatic unlocking of the next episode. This lets you continue watching and keep your unlocked episodes on any device where you sign in.

Eligible free episodes can also be opened through a direct episode link without signing in. Progress saved without signing in is stored on our servers under a random identifier kept on your device. This history is separate from your account: deleting the account or clearing app storage does not erase it from our servers.

Coins and purchases

Coin purchases are processed by Google Play. RevenueCat verifies each purchase for us. We handle the coin pack bought, purchase and transaction references, the purchase status, coins added or spent, the episodes unlocked with coins, the price in coins at the time, and related dates and times. Our servers, not the app, decide your coin balance and which episodes you can watch. We never receive your full payment card number.

Device and technical information

When the app connects to our services, we and our providers process information needed to deliver and secure the service, such as your IP address, request times, app version and technical identifiers. The app supports Firebase App Check with Google Play Integrity to help verify app requests when enabled; its use depends on the test build and service configuration. The app stores a random device identifier and, while a purchase or unlock is being completed, its reference in encrypted storage on your phone so it can finish safely if the app is interrupted. These do not contain payment card details.

Optional analytics

Analytics is off unless you turn it on in the app's privacy settings. If you turn it on, Google Firebase Analytics receives app usage events such as app opens, sign-in method, episodes started and finished, playback errors, app version, language, optional country and random identifiers for your session, account, series and episodes. You can turn it off at any time; this stops collection and clears the analytics identifier on your phone.

Support emails

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write or attach. The app only opens a draft email when you ask it to, and never attaches passwords, receipts or hidden account identifiers. Please do not send passwords, verification codes or payment card details.

What we do not collect

Stovio does not ask for your precise location, contacts, photos or microphone, and does not sell your personal information or share it for advertising.

Why we use information, and our legal bases

  • To provide Stovio (performance of our contract with you): creating and securing your account, streaming episodes, saving progress, selling coins, keeping your coin balance, unlocking episodes and answering support requests.
  • With your consent: optional analytics. Saying no does not limit any feature.
  • To meet legal obligations: keeping purchase and accounting records and answering privacy requests.
  • For our legitimate interests: keeping the service secure and reliable, preventing fraud and duplicate charges, and establishing or defending legal claims.

Who receives information

We use the following providers to run Stovio. Each receives the information needed for the services listed here. Their roles, terms and processing locations are still being reviewed for the release:

  • Google (Firebase Authentication, App Check and optional Analytics; Google Play for app distribution and payments; Google Cloud for our servers; Gmail for support email);
  • Supabase, for our database;
  • RevenueCat, for purchase verification;
  • Bunny.net, for video delivery.
  • GitHub Pages, for hosting these public information pages.

We may also disclose information when the law requires it, or to protect our users or the service.

International transfers

Some of these providers may process information outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. The applicable locations, contracts and transfer safeguards are still under review for the release. We have not yet verified a transfer safeguard for each provider.

How long we keep information

The periods described as targets below have been selected, but cleanup and provider settings have not yet been verified for every category. They are not currently guaranteed maximum storage periods.

  • Account, preferences and signed-in viewing activity: kept while the account exists and removed from our active database following verified account deletion. Automatic inactivity cleanup is not yet enabled. Our target is to give notice after 24 months without sign-in or purchase activity, then remove nonessential preferences and viewing history while preserving access to purchased episodes.
  • Viewing activity saved without signing in: stored separately under the device identifier. The target is deletion 12 months after the last saved episode progress, but scheduled expiry is not yet enabled.
  • Coin and purchase records: retained for accounting, refunds, chargebacks, disputes and prevention of duplicate transactions. Account deletion separates these records from the sign-in account; it does not erase them. The required retention period and deletion or anonymization schedule are still under review.
  • Proof that an account was deleted: a minimal record without your name or email is currently retained without automatic expiry. The target is three years after deletion is completed. Pending sign-in cleanup keeps the account identifier until that cleanup succeeds.
  • Support emails: the target is 12 months after your request is closed, subject to justified legal or dispute holds. Mailbox expiry settings have not yet been verified.
  • Server logs: the target is six months, or up to 12 months for a specific security incident. Provider-controlled mandatory audit logs can have longer periods; the complete set of provider settings is still under review.
  • Backups: the target is a rolling 35-day expiry. Backup expiry and removal of deleted data after a restore have not yet been verified.
  • Optional analytics: the target is 14 months from collection, without extending it when you return. Provider retention settings and deletion of historical events have not yet been verified.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app: open Account, then Account management, then Delete account. You can also request deletion without the app. See Delete your Stovio account for the steps and for what is deleted and kept. Removing your Stovio profile does not confirm that sign-in cleanup or deletion by every provider has finished. Purchase records and historical analytics can remain with providers after local account deletion.

Your rights

Under data protection law, including the GDPR, you can ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, to restrict or object to its use, and to receive a copy in a portable format. You can withdraw consent to optional analytics at any time. To make a request, email pharaujo1094@gmail.com. We may ask for information to confirm that the account is yours, and we answer within one month.

You can also complain to the French data protection authority, the CNIL, or to the authority where you live.

Children

If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

We use encrypted connections, short-lived video access and server-side checks for purchases, coin balances and unlocks. Purchases and paid unlocks require a signed-in account. No system can be perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when Stovio or the way it handles information changes, and change the date at the top. For significant changes we will also tell you in the app.
