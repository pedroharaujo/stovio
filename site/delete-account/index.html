Delete your Stovio account

Last updated: 27 September 2026

This page explains how to delete your account in the Stovio Android app, operated by Pedro Henrique Araujo Pinto, and what happens to your data.

Option 1: in the app

  1. Open Stovio and sign in.
  2. Open Account.
  3. Open Account management.
  4. Tap Delete account.
  5. Confirm with your password, or with the same Google account you use to sign in.

Your profile and its active account data are removed from Stovio's database and you are signed out. Your sign-in account is normally removed at the same time; if that step is delayed, cleanup remains pending. This does not confirm that every provider has finished deleting data. The records described below can remain.

Option 2: without the app

  1. Email pharaujo1094@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Stovio account.
  2. Send it from the email address you use to sign in to Stovio (for Google sign-in, that Google account's address).
  3. We may reply to confirm the account is yours. We never ask for your password, verification codes or payment card details.

We delete the account within one month of confirming the request, and email you when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your Stovio sign-in account (email/password or Google sign-in link), once sign-in cleanup finishes.
  • Your profile, language, country and privacy choices.
  • Your signed-in watch history, playback positions and settings such as automatic unlocking.
  • Your access to episodes you unlocked, and any remaining coins.

Deletion cannot be undone. Remaining coins and unlocked episodes cannot be recovered or moved to another account.

History saved without signing in is stored separately under a random device identifier. Deleting your account or clearing app storage does not erase that history from our servers. Contact us about requests involving this history.

What is kept, and for how long

Stovio is currently being tested. The target periods below have been selected, but their enforcement is not yet verified and they are not guaranteed maximum storage periods.

  • Coin purchase and spending records, separated from your sign-in account: retained for accounting, refunds, chargebacks, disputes and prevention of duplicate transactions. The required retention period and deletion or anonymization schedule are still under review.
  • A record that the deletion happened, without your name or email: currently retained without automatic expiry. The target is three years after completion. Pending sign-in cleanup keeps the account identifier until cleanup succeeds.
  • Support emails: the target is 12 months after your request is closed, subject to justified legal or dispute holds. Mailbox expiry settings have not yet been verified.
  • Backups: the target is a rolling 35-day expiry. Actual expiry and removal of deleted data after a restore have not yet been verified.

Google Play keeps its own record of your purchases under Google's privacy policy. RevenueCat, which verifies purchases for us, holds purchase records linked to a random identifier rather than your name or email. Removing your Stovio profile does not confirm that RevenueCat records or historical analytics events have been deleted by those providers.
